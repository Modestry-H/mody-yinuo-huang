About

Hospitality is the business of making people feel considered.

I am a third-year student of International Integrated Resort Management at the University of Macau. Over the past two years I have planned more than twenty campus events, produced thirty-plus photo and video assignments for the university's Communications Office, and interned in hotel operations and public service.

I am drawn to the part of hospitality that never appears on a programme: the timing, the signage, the person who answers before you ask. I would like to build my career in hotel operations, guest experience and events.

I am warm, steady and genuinely interested in people. At work I am proactive and quick to learn, I take responsibility and stay calm under pressure, and I communicate openly with colleagues and guests. Disciplined, adaptable and creative, I am at my best inside a team.
